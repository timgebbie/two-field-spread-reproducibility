\textbf{Status.} The Markov pilot and execution tape are implemented and tested.
Long-run LMF diagnostics, convergence acceptance, fractional transport and
calendar subordination are not completed. This draft describes the actual
software; it does not amend the supplied theory paper or letter.

\section{Model, agents and the inherited numerical construction}
The state is two nonnegative densities, $\rho_B(x_j,u_n)$ and
$\rho_A(x_j,u_n)$, in one market. A cell carries volume $\rho_i\dx$;
$x$ is log price. Fixed endpoint reservoirs are excluded from executable
volume. Lit and latent sources aggregate supply; diffusion aggregates
repricing; cancellation removes standing volume; symmetric reaction removes
overlap. These are an aggregate closure with an order-level random-walk
interpretation. The current solver does not simulate individual traders,
wealth, beliefs or strategy choice.

The market-making sector is represented by pending completion stocks and
source placement. All explicitly executed child volume in this pilot is
attributed to that sector. Its opposite-side source is
\emph{completion-equivalent}: survival and execution probabilities are
assumed inside the completion/placement specification. Source delivery is
neither a new trade print nor independently observed settlement. Reaction
loss is accounted for as a field sink, without inventing aggressor signs or
prices for it. Thus the trade tape records the specified aggressor programme,
not every possible microscopic match.

Let $Q_B$ denote bid replenishment owed after sales and $Q_A$ ask
replenishment owed after purchases. The quote-setting sample obeys
\begin{align}
 Q_\Sigma&=Q_A+Q_B,& Q_\Delta&=Q_A-Q_B,\\
 \Sigma&=\Sigma_0+\chi_s Q_\Sigma,&
 \mu&=\mu_0-\chi_m Q_\Delta,\qquad
 q_b=\mu-\Sigma/2,\quad q_a=\mu+\Sigma/2.
\end{align}
Observed quotes $p_b,p_a$ are side-density threshold crossings, not $q_b,q_a$.
Zero signed inventory does not imply zero total pending exposure. Also,
$\Sigma_0=12$ is imposed in this pilot: the existence of its baseline spread
is not evidence of spontaneous spread formation from zero standoff.

Diana--Gebbie explicitly identify the need for two densities to represent a
nonzero spread \cite{cam}. The correlation-emergence software supplies the
operational DTRW, execution-record and observation conventions \cite{ag,code}.
Its two coupled books are not identified with the two sides of this market.
The new density core implements the supplied spread paper's Markov branch.
For the common reaction-free interior operator, the prototype has
\[
 \rho^{n+1}=e^{-\nu\du}\rho^n+\tfrac r2\lap\rho^n+\du S^n,
 \qquad r=2D\du/\dx^2,
\]
where $\lap\rho_j=\rho_{j-1}-2\rho_j+\rho_{j+1}$. Here cancellation is
$(1-\nu\du)\rho^n$. Their difference is
$(e^{-\nu\du}-1+\nu\du)\rho^n$, of one-step order $\du^2$.
Matched transport agrees to roundoff at $\nu=\kappa=0$; complete trajectories
are not asserted identical. The prototype consumes before its field update;
the present paper convention consumes after it. Phase labels preserve this
distinction. See \texttt{compatibility-v0.4.1.json} for the executed comparison.

\section{Algorithm 1: implemented Markov update}
\textbf{Inputs:} previous post-event densities, post-event pending stocks
$P_B,P_A$, fixed $\dx,\du$, parameters and event-integrated requests.
Both sides read one frozen field; new executions cannot complete immediately.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Deliver old pending cohorts:
$C_i=(1-e^{-\du/\tau_h})P_i$ and $Q_i=P_i-C_i$.
For the next-update control set $C_i=P_i$. This is the only
``immediate'' convention used. Form $q_b,q_a$ from $Q$ above.
\item Evaluate the paper's lit and latent sources $S_i^{\rm ext}$ on the
outward side of $q_i$. Normalize uniform nodal weights $g_B$ on
$[q_b-w,q_b]$ and $g_A$ on $[q_a,q_a+w]$ so that $\dx\sum_jg_{i,j}=1$.
Reject empty or reservoir-truncated supports. No supply is placed between
the placement edges.
\item For interior nodes, compute the pre-event field
\[
 \widetilde\rho_{i,j}=(1-2a-\nu\du-\kappa\du\rho_{\bar i,j})\rho_{i,j}
 +a(\rho_{i,j-1}+\rho_{i,j+1})+\du S_{i,j}^{\rm ext}+C_i g_{i,j},
 \quad a=D\du/\dx^2.
\]
Require $2a+\nu\du+\kappa\du\rho_{\bar i,j}\leq1$ everywhere.
Keep endpoint reservoirs fixed; reject inadmissible states without clipping.
\item Read pre-event quotes by linear interpolation at density threshold
$\epsilon$: the rightmost downward bid crossing and leftmost upward ask
crossing. Record crossing counts; reject a selected plateau, insufficient
selected slope, reservoir failure or contact.
\item Execute each request in outward price priority, starting at its
pre-event quote and ending at the configured execution depth. Restrict to
interior node centres. Remove at most $\widetilde\rho_{i,j}\dx$ per node;
record actual fills, requests and unfilled demand. No reservoir is consumed.
The current tape requires one aggressor side per event.
\item Let $v_A$ be actual buy volume and $v_B$ actual sell volume. Set
$P_B^{\rm new}=Q_B+v_A$, $P_A^{\rm new}=Q_A+v_B$.
Read post-event quotes and save both inventory samples, actual removals and
separate source increments. An event at $u$ is applied at the end of the
interval ending at $u$; pre/post fields share $u$ with different phase labels.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Checks on every update.} For each side, the interior volume change is
boundary flux minus cancellation, minus symmetric reaction, plus external
supply, plus completion, minus execution. The boundary increment is
$D\du(\rho_0-\rho_1+\rho_J-\rho_{J-1})/\dx$. Reaction loss is
$\kappa\du\dx\sum_j\rho_{A,j}\rho_{B,j}$ on each side. It cancels from
imbalance and occurs twice in total density. Cumulative initiation must equal
cumulative completion plus post-event pending stock, separately by side.

\textbf{Initialization and recovery.} Relax the same positive-reaction equations
with empty pending stock and no executions to the configured residual.
Recompute geometric completion weights when $\du$ changes, retaining physical
$\tau_h$. Preserve pending tails beyond a finite plotting horizon.
Volume-balanced round trips need not restore liquidity at the quote at the
same time: delivery occurs elsewhere and transport takes time. Neither the
ledger nor a stationary field establishes dealer profitability or a strategic
equilibrium. Hard source supports require grid-phase refinement; the current
late spread offset has not been accepted as physical.

\section{Algorithm 2: implemented execution tape}
\begin{enumerate}
\item For event $n$ retain nonzero executed node volumes $v_{n,j}$, parent
identifier, aggressor sign $\varepsilon_n$ ($+1$ buy, $-1$ sell), operational
index and time. No fill means no trade observation. Preserve each fill row
and the requested/unfilled amounts in the event record.
\item Following the prior executable convention, compute the primary
execution log price
\[
 z_n=\frac{\sum_jv_{n,j}x_j}{\sum_jv_{n,j}}.
\]
Store $e^{z_n}$ as the geometric execution price and
$\sum_jv_{n,j}e^{x_j}/\sum_jv_{n,j}$ as the separate arithmetic price VWAP.
Do not call these identical. Store pre/post bid, ask, log midpoint and spread,
and the placement and pending state that generated the event.
\item Preserve the true aggressor sign separately from the quote sign
$\operatorname{sign}(z_n-m_n^{\rm pre})$ and tick sign
$\operatorname{sign}(z_n-z_{n-1})$. The first tick label is zero;
a zero tick subsequently carries the last nonzero tick label, as in v2.0.0.
\item Using consecutive executed events, calculate
$r^m_n=m_n^{\rm post}-m_{n-1}^{\rm post}$,
$r^T_n=z_n-z_{n-1}$ and
$\Delta s_n=s_n^{\rm post}-s_{n-1}^{\rm post}$.
Retain absolute increments. First increments are missing, not zero.
An instantaneous pre/post midpoint jump is stored separately from $r^m_n$,
which also includes inter-event field evolution.
\end{enumerate}
Prices are measured from actual density consumption; there is no independent
synthetic price process. The current six same-sign children demonstrate the
measurement path only. Their sign variance is zero, so no sign ACF or LMF
claim is available from this run.

\section{Algorithm 3: lagged correlation}
The estimator is implemented and tested; the long-run protocol below has
not yet been executed. For aligned finite series $X,Y$, define
\[
 \widehat C_{XY}(k)=
 \frac{\sum_{n\in I_k}(X_n-\bar X_k)(Y_{n+k}-\bar Y_k)}
 {\sqrt{\sum_{n\in I_k}(X_n-\bar X_k)^2
              \sum_{n\in I_k}(Y_{n+k}-\bar Y_k)^2}}.
\]
Each mean is taken over its own overlapping slice. Positive $k$ means $X$
leads $Y$. Save pair counts, lag units and phase alignment. Zero variance is
undefined, not zero correlation. Do not silently compress missing observations.
This reproduces the v2.0.0 executable Pearson ACF. Its supplementary text
instead prints a global-mean covariance normalization; that documentation
mismatch is not carried into this supplement.

For future runs, retain raw price paths but use $r^m,r^T,|r^m|,|r^T|$ for
price ACFs. For a positive price, $|P|=P$; an absolute log-price level depends
on the arbitrary origin. Spread-level ACFs require a checked stationary
window. Price-level ACFs, if retained, must be labelled descriptive rather
than evidence of long memory. A fixed valuation centre and fixed reservoirs
can cause mean reversion; diffusive prices are not guaranteed.

\section{Next numerical experiments: isolate the market maker}
No chartist or fundamentalist population is required for the current spread
question. Aggregate source functions can represent their net activity only
after a specified behavioural closure. Existing stationary sources do not
already encode trend following, valuation switching or persistent order signs.
The fixed $\mu_0$ is a valuation reference, not a simulated fundamentalist.

\begin{enumerate}
\item First resolve grid, time-step, domain, threshold and support-phase
sensitivity for the saved pilot. Retain the supplied equations and physical
event volumes; do not smooth the source to force attractive plots.
\item Reuse the same input tape in delayed and next-update completion controls.
Separate width feedback ($\chi_s=0$) from centre feedback ($\chi_m=0$);
the existing jointly frozen control cannot identify them individually.
Retain equal actual filled volumes or report that a comparison failed.
\item Add a matched balanced buy/sell programme. Show both $Q_\Sigma$ and
$Q_\Delta$, so small signed inventory with material pending exposure can be
distinguished from an empty book. Show $q_b,q_a$ separately from $p_b,p_a$.
During sustained flow, test stable pending exposure and flow balance; after
stopping, show remaining completion and density recovery. Do not describe
source delivery as a second executed leg.
\item Introduce a minimal aggregate order-splitting generator, with stored
parent IDs, independent centred parent signs and an explicitly specified
length law. For the LMF benchmark use a parent survival tail
$\Pr(L\geq\ell)\sim\ell^{-\beta}$, $1<\beta<2$. Under its order-splitting
assumptions the sign ACF exponent is $\gamma=\beta-1$ \cite{lmf}.
Consecutive same-sign parents remain distinct. Register initialization,
finite-size truncation, run length, lag window, seeds and independent
replicates before inspecting correlation slopes. Match an independent-sign
null at the same child volume and rate. A run generator is an aggregate
order-flow closure, not evidence of endogenous LMF generation by RD.
\item Apply that tape to the density solver. Measure ACFs of
$\varepsilon,r^m,r^T,|r^m|,|r^T|,s$. Measure cross-correlations of
$(\varepsilon,r^m)$, $(\varepsilon,\Delta s)$, $(r^m,r^T)$,
$(r^m,\Delta s)$, $(|r^m|,s)$ and $(|r^T|,s)$.
Use independent path groups for uncertainty; reflected paths are paired,
not additional independent replicates. Check stationarity and censoring;
retain all inadmissible exits rather than selecting only successful paths.
\end{enumerate}
Signed midpoint--spread correlation may cancel by buy/sell symmetry;
magnitude correlations can still show coupling. Bid/ask bounce may affect
short-lag trade returns. Neither is a result established by the current pilot.

\textbf{Focused figure target.} Keep F2 density snapshots, F3 prices and
market-maker state (now including actual execution prices), F4 matched
mechanism controls and V1 profile video. Add F5: a three-by-three layout with
three aligned path panels and the six ACFs. Add F6: the six cross-correlations
in square panels. These are numerical outputs from retained trajectories,
not a separate theoretical illustration set. Exact panels can share axes
and controls without adding a gallery of unrelated stylised facts.

\textbf{Square-root impact.} A single small-volume programme establishes no
impact exponent. Any subsequent scaling test must register a matched
no-programme baseline, actual executed volume, duration and participation
controls, a valid scale window and convergence checks. A pre-contact,
finite-threshold spread model does not inherit the single-front square-root
limit automatically. The minimal reaction--diffusion impact literature
provides a comparison target \cite{impact}, not a universal acceptance
criterion. Broad impact scaling remains a later extension.

\section{Future memory and clock compatibility}
Keep four mechanisms distinct: event-time order splitting ($\beta$),
operational transport memory ($\alpha_u$), round-trip completion ($h,\tau_h$)
and calendar observation/subordination ($\alpha_t$). Give stochastic
components separate recorded seed streams. The event/operational/calendar
distinction also follows the companion account \cite{clocks}; its abstract
provides context here, not verification of a fractional two-side solver.

The prior raw Sibuya kernel is
\[
 K_1=\alpha_u,\qquad K_2=\tfrac12\alpha_u(\alpha_u-1),\qquad
 K_k=K_{k-1}\left(1-\frac{2-\alpha_u}{k}\right),\ k\geq3.
\]
The prototype applies survival $e^{-\nu(k-1)\du}$ to lagged transport
and $e^{-\nu\du}$ to the current survivor, once each. Cancellation is not
already folded into the raw kernel. $\alpha_u=1$ leaves only $K_1=1$.
Its scaling is $D_{\alpha_u}=r\dx^2/(2\du^{\alpha_u})$; changing
$\alpha_u$ while silently retaining an incompatible $r,\du,D$ is invalid.

The current state has no transport-history buffer. A future fractional
two-side model must derive the survival and history treatment of nonlinear
reaction, cancellation, births and consumed volume. It must not resurrect
removed liquidity through old history. This requires a stated formulation
and tests against the prior primitive and its Markov limit; inserting the
kernel into the existing Laplacian alone is insufficient.

The paper's stationary placement construction requires a finite mean
completion lag. A heavy-tailed transport waiting time is not permission to
replace the completion density with an infinite-mean law. That would change
the equilibrium assumptions and can make pending exposure diverge under
sustained input.

The correlation-emergence observation layer acts on completed operational
paths. A future projection must select the previous completed state under
the declared clock, retain index maps and reject out-of-support queries.
Do not interpolate across an execution. Use a coherent book observation for
bid, ask and midpoint before testing separate reporting delays. A held last
trade price creates neither another trade nor another sign. Empty calendar
bins have zero signed flow, not a repeated aggressor; distinguish them from
missing pre-first-trade observations. Compare shocked/control paths using
the same realized clock. These rules are specified, not yet implemented.
